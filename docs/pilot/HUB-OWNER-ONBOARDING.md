# Real hub owner onboarding

Do this with 1–3 consenting real Lahore venue owners. Do not seed public inventory. F0 disposable records are for acceptance and must be removed afterward.

1. Sign up or log in and complete Player Setup with the owner's actual name, email, phone and home address. Upload a profile photo if desired.
2. In Profile choose Activate Hub Owner, complete Hub Setup, and switch to Ground Owner mode.
3. Open Venue details & photos. Enter the actual venue name, street address, Lahore area, contact number, supported sports and description. Place the pin at the venue entrance inside Lahore. Upload an owned, accurate venue photograph. Save and reopen to check persistence.
4. Open Playing spaces & availability. Add each actual pitch/court separately, with its ground/hall name, sport, format, surface, indoor/outdoor setting and capacity. Avoid duplicate names.
5. Add a future slot in Pakistan time, with its full-slot PKR price. Verify end is later than start and it does not overlap another slot on the same space.
6. Disclose the full/advance payment option, advance percentage, cancellation deadline and full-refund/keep-advance rule. Late cancellation gives no refund. In this pilot `PAYMENT_MODE=disabled`: arrange directly with the venue; never claim a confirmed paid Maidaan reservation. Sandbox tests collect no funds.
7. Submit verification. An administrator reviews the details and records approval/rejection. Approval means administrative review only; it is not a physical inspection or safety certification.
8. Use a separate Player account to search the venue by name and area. Check photos, pin, court details, prices and available slots. Correct any inaccurate information before inviting participants.

Completion record: owner consent, date, verified contact, venue address/pin checked by owner, photo permission, administrator review note, and any follow-up issues. Never record passwords or API secrets here.
