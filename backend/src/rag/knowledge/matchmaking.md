# Match Discovery & Matchmaking Workflow

## The Matchmaking Lifecycle
Matchmaking in Maidaan connects team captains who have open player slots with solo athletes looking for games. The complete workflow operates through the following steps:
1. Match Creation: A Team Captain publishes an open fixture specifying the sport, date, start time, venue, neighborhood, skill level, total capacity, open slots, and entry contribution per player.
2. Discovery: Players discover open fixtures using either the interactive schematic ground map or the filterable list view on the Discover tab.
3. Match Inspection: Players tap a fixture to view complete squad lineup details, venue address, and live weather suitability indicators.
4. Join Request: An interested player submits a join request with an optional introductory message to the captain.
5. Captain Evaluation: The hosting captain reviews the player's profile and accepts or rejects the request.
6. Spot Confirmation: Upon acceptance, the player receives an in-app confirmation ticket, the open slots count decrements atomically, and squad group chat access is unlocked.

## Supported Matchmaking Sports
Maidaan supports match organization for four sports:
- Cricket: Hard-ball, tape-ball, and box cricket fixtures with customizable player counts.
- Football: Futsal, 5-a-side, 7-a-side, and 11-a-side matches.
- Badminton: Singles and doubles court bookings.
- Padel: 4-player padel court matches.

## Real Fixtures vs Automatic Booking
Maidaan does not operate as an automated commercial venue checkout system. All matches represent human-organized recreational games hosted by team captains. Spots are subject to explicit captain review and live slot availability.
